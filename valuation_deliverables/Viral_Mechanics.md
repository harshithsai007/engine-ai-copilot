# Viral Loop Mechanics for EngineAI

This document outlines the viral growth strategies for EngineAI, leveraging marketing psychology to reach the 17,000-user milestone.

## 1. Incentives (Reciprocity & Reward)
**Mechanism:** The "Double-Sided Reward" system.
- **For the Referrer:** Every successful referral grants 500 extra "Compute Tokens" or access to premium LLM models for 48 hours.
- **For the Referee:** New users joining via a referral link start with a "Jumpstart Pack" (20% more initial credits).
- **Psychology:** Taps into the principle of reciprocity. Users feel a social obligation to share when they get a tangible benefit that also helps their network.

## 2. The IKEA Effect (Labor = Love)
**Mechanism:** "Custom Agent Crafting."
- **Implementation:** EngineAI allows users to fine-tune their own "Expert Agents" by providing personal data or specific logic rules.
- **Viral Trigger:** Once a user spends 30+ minutes configuring an agent, they are prompted to "Publish" it to the EngineAI Community Gallery. 
- **Psychology:** People value products more when they have put effort into creating them. By letting users build and show off their custom agents, we increase retention and create "shareable units" of work.

## 3. Social Proof (Validation & Trust)
**Mechanism:** "Engineered by [User Name]" Signature.
- **Implementation:** Every output (code, report, or image) generated by EngineAI includes a subtle but professional "Engineered with EngineAI" badge.
- **Community Feed:** A live "Success Stream" showing real-time problems being solved by the community (e.g., "User X just saved 4 hours of coding with the Python-Architect agent").
- **Psychology:** When people see others in their niche using a tool to succeed, they experience FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) and trust the product's efficacy before even trying it.

## 4. The Loop Lifecycle
1. **Creation:** User builds a custom agent (IKEA Effect).
2. **Success:** User achieves a task and sees the badge (Social Proof).
3. **Sharing:** User shares the output or the agent link to their network.
4. **Reward:** User receives credits when others join (Incentive).
5. **Growth:** New users join, and the cycle repeats.
